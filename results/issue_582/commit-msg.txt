fix(merge): confirm a green check rollup before merging a PR

In merge mode, the wave waits for a PR's checks and merges on the first
green rollup it reads. Seconds after a PR is opened, the rollup lists
only the checks that have registered so far. A fast check that already
passed (a DCO or issue-link check) reads as green while a slow job has
not created its check run yet. The wave then merges on that partial
green. If the slow job is a required check, `gh pr merge` refuses and the
wave stops. If it is not required, which is the case
`merge_requires = "all"` exists for, the PR merges and the check fails
afterwards on the base branch.

A green read is now confirmed before it is believed. The wait reads the
rollup again one full poll interval (15 s) later, charged to
`merge_wait_secs`, and merges only if that read is green too. A confirm
read that is pending or empty goes back into the wait. A failing or
unreadable one refuses at once and returns the PR to draft, as a failing
rollup already does. The confirm is never shortened to fit the budget:
a green first seen with less than 15 s of budget left is refused as not
finished, with a message that says the green could not be confirmed.
Total wait time still never exceeds `merge_wait_secs`.

Behaviour change for small budgets: `merge_wait_secs` from 1 to 14 now
refuses every PR under `merge_requires = "all"`, and with 15 a rollup
that turns green only after one wait is refused. Raise the bound if your
checks are slow to report. `merge_wait_secs = 0` is unchanged: one read,
no wait. The comments for `merge_wait_secs` in pdca.toml and config.py
describe the new rule.

This narrows the problem but does not close it. The confirm compares the
verdict only, not which checks are present, so a slow job that has not
registered within 15 s still gets through. Closing that needs a check-name
comparison or a configured list of expected checks.
