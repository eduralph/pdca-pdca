flow: hold a dependent whose line lacks its merged prerequisite

In stack mode, a bundle that depends on a prerequisite outside the run
(a "Depends on (merged)" edge, or a plain "Depends on" the run was not
asked for) waits until that prerequisite's PR has merged into its base.
But once the run has an integration line for that base, the dependent is
cut from the line, not from the base, and the line does not follow the
base. If the prerequisite's PR merged after the line was started, the
dependent passed the check and was built, verified and published on a
line without the prerequisite's change, with no warning.

The gate now also asks whether the line tip the run recorded has the
prerequisite's work: the commit its PR merge put on the base
(mergeCommit, which covers merge, squash and rebase merges), or the PR's
head (which the resume carry puts on a line it continues). If neither is
there, the dependent is held and named on one stderr line with the line
and its tip, and told the way out: once the run ends, delete the line on
origin, then re-run. If the merge commit cannot be read or the checkout
cannot be fetched, the dependent is held too. A target with no line yet,
and merge mode, behave as before.

merged.py gains merge_and_head(), and pr_state() now shares one gh pr
view helper with it. docs/07-crosscutting.md describes the new hold.

This closes the gap listed under "Known limits" of
1b6831b02fb1f413c8f9f24f653ef0ae4a928870, whose plain-edge hold reuses
the same check.
