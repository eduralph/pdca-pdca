The exit-code claim in (ii) holds: a skipped BUILT entry is not in `_FLOW_OK` (`cli.py:669`, `:683`). Five concrete gaps remain:

- NEEDS-HUMAN — **Writing the brief before the bundle is claimed breaks the #565 claim rule on two of the three intake paths.** The brief says the repair happens "Before the bundle enters the run" but never says it must come after the bundle is claimed.
  - **`--from-csv` sweep:** the filter at `flow.py:1929-1934` runs before `_claim_swept` (`flow.py:1939-1940`).
  - **Adoption:** `_adoptable`'s no-brief filter (`flow.py:1104-1108`) runs before the adopted child is claimed. The caller, `_adopt_split_children`, claims "where it is reached".
  - **Why it matters:** `cli.py:582-584` says implicitly reached bundles are "claimed where [they are] reached", and `flow_batch`'s docstring (`flow.py:1904-1907`) says a bundle another live run holds is "named and left alone". A fix that rewrites `brief.md` inside the sweep or adoption filter would write into a bundle another driver may hold.
  - **Suggested revision:** say that on the sweep and adoption paths the repair runs only after the claim succeeds (an unclaimable bundle is excluded, not repaired). Add a (iii) test case where another run holds the bundle, so the `brief.md` must not appear.
  - Only `flow_ids` is safe as written, because `cli._flow_claimed` claims named ids first (`cli.py:602-615`).

- NEEDS-HUMAN — **The child list as written cannot produce a byte-identical brief.** Success criterion (i) says "the child list is the parent's lineage `children`". But `split.accept` passes bundle names: `[cfg.bundle(i).name for i in ids]` (`split.py:939`), e.g. `issue_701`. Read literally, the brief makes Do pass `701, 702` and miss byte-identity. Say "`cfg.bundle(c).name` for each lineage child, in record order".

- NEEDS-HUMAN — **No outcome is given when the archive is usable but the lineage is not.** (i) needs the lineage `children`. (ii) covers only "no archive, or an archive `_parent_plan` refuses". "Missing lineage" is listed as out of scope, but the brief never says what happens to a briefless split parent whose `split-lineage.json` is missing, unreadable, or has an empty or garbage `children` list (`flow.py:741-752` shows hand-edited records are an expected case). Do will have to choose between writing a brief that names no children (not what any accept wrote), skipping, or crashing.
