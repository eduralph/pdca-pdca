flow: stop holding a bundle for a merge its own base already has

In stack mode, a bundle whose out-of-batch prerequisite merged into main
after the run's first fold was held with "does not have that merge",
even though it would be built on a base of its own (or on the real base)
that is fetched after the check and so already holds that merge. The
user had to delete the run's line and re-run for nothing.

The run's line is now checked only for bundles that are still built on
it: those a seam keeps there (a `Stacks on` or `Onto branch` edge, or a
carried prerequisite). Every other bundle is judged by whether the
prerequisite merged into its target base, which is the tree it is built
on. Whether a bundle is on the line comes from the same per-wave plan
that later points it at its base, so the two cannot disagree.

The line-based tests keep their held assertions, now with a dependent
that a seam keeps on the line. A new test file covers the own-base and
real-base cases and the still-unmerged guard.

Fixes #729
