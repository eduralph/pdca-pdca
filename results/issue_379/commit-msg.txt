Tell each headless leaf the harness owns its working files

The role bodies of the reviewer, the two advisory reviewers, the plan
advisory and the builder never said where those leaves may write, or who
cleans up afterwards. The harness has always owned that lifecycle: the
review sandboxes are temporary directories it deletes when the leaf exits,
and the builder's worktree is created and reclaimed by the driver. Left
unstated, a careful model fills the gap itself — it makes a scratch
directory somewhere outside the space it was given, and then feels obliged
to delete it again.

That guess costs real runs. A vendor sandbox that refuses rm-style commands
rejects the entire command such a cleanup step rides on, before any of it
executes, so the validation the model attached it to is lost and has to be
retried; when the model gives up instead, the invented directory is left
behind under /tmp or /var/tmp.

Each of the five bodies now states its own filesystem contract: which roots
the harness gives it to write in, that it must not create files anywhere
else, and that reclaiming those roots is the harness's job — so no rm-style
command is ever warranted. The roots are phrased as "the roots the harness
gives you" and then enumerated, so a root the harness may hand out later is
covered without the wording having to change or contradict itself. No
persistent or cached scratch location is named, promised, or implied.

The two shapes differ on purpose: the sandboxed leaves are told their cwd
(with the target source read-only), while the builder is told its worktree
and the bundle directory it is granted, because it must keep writing its
artifacts there. The text lives in the vendor-neutral bodies under
agents/, so every vendor inherits it, and sits outside every conditional so
it renders for every instance.
