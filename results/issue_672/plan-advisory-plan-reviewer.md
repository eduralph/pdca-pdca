The findings left are gaps, not a wrong framing:

- NEEDS-HUMAN — **On a continued line, a skipped `P` can still vouch for its old commits, and the brief does not say so.** The Definition exempts every commit reachable from "the commit the fold started the line from (… origin's line tip on a continued one)". The carry decides whether to continue origin's line by letting each state-clean finished id's head vouch for the line's commits (`flow.py:1094-1095`: `foreign_commit(repo, tip, base_ref, [h for n, h in heads.items() if n not in why])`). So: an earlier run's line holds `p1`, and `P` has since been updated with a conflicting `p2`. `P` is state-clean, so its head vouches for `p1` and the line is continued. Then `P`'s merge fails at fold time (skipped), yet `p1` stays on the line the carry pushes and `E` builds on it. That is a fold-time-skipped id's work on the pushed line, which the brief's own Invariant prose says the carry "never puts onto the line". The Definition exempts it without saying so, and the out-of-scope list (i)–(iv) does not name it. The rewrite of `docs/07-crosscutting.md:728-730` could then claim more than is true. Ask the planner to name this case: either as an explicit known limit in the new doc sentence and the out-of-scope list, or by having a fold-time skip withdraw that id's vouch from the `foreign_commit` check (a scope decision).

- NEEDS-HUMAN — **"The head the fold read for `P`" is not available to the carry, and for one kind of skip it does not exist.** `fold` reads each head inside its loop (`integrate.py:520`) and exposes only `took` (merged ones) via `carried`. The carry already holds a different head for `P`: `heads[P]`, the PR head from `gh` (`flow.py:1084-1088`). In the test stub that head always equals the branch tip (`test_flow_resume_stack_prereqs.py:99`), so no criterion can tell the two apart. The brief says the Definition is "not Do's to refine", yet Do has to choose which head to use. The brief should say which one, and what happens when the fold read no head: fall back to `heads[P]`, or hold every holder conservatively.

- NEEDS-HUMAN — **"Skip" covers more than a conflict, but every criterion is a conflict.** Under `skipped=`, any `IntegrationError` from `_merge_published` is a skip, including the non-conflict `git merge` failure (`integrate.py:632-635`) and the gone-branch errors (`:661-686`).
