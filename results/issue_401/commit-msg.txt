fix(gates): record a Check-time T4 row as deferred, not a pass

The contribution gate lints the commit message and the PR body, and those two
files are drafted at publish — after Check has already run. At Check time the
gate therefore had nothing to lint, exited 0, and the matrix recorded a plain
green with an empty evidence string: a pass nobody could reproduce, because the
artifacts the row names do not exist yet. The reviewer's only honest move was to
mark the row provisional, so every cycle ended with the same T4 item sitting in
SUMMARY.md §6 NEEDS-HUMAN — 9 of 9 frozen bundles in this instance's last review
— cleared unread every time. An item that fires on 100% of cycles trains the
human to tick §6 boxes, which is the guard C6 depends on.

A gate that runs and finds its subject absent by design now says so. It prints
`PDCA-DEFERRED: <reason>` while exiting 0, and the row is recorded `deferred`:
visible in the matrix and in §5 evidence with its reason, counted neither as a
green nor as a gating failure, and — the one deliberate difference from
`unverifiable` — not lifted into §6. The two differ in who owes the answer:
`unverifiable` means nobody has one, so a human must decide; `deferred` means
this row's substantive audit runs later, at a gate that cannot be skipped, so
there is nothing for the human to clear.

A deferral is a hand-off, never a waiver. The marker is honoured only for a row
something genuinely re-runs later — one the publish re-gate selects (a
bundle-scoped T4 row, or an explicit `at_publish = true`) — so no audit can be
dropped by printing a line, and a row nothing re-gates keeps its pass/fail. The
substantive T4 verdict is unchanged and still hard-gates the push.

Upgrading an existing instance: nothing rewrites a frozen check-gates.json, and
readers that test for `fail` or `unverifiable` are unaffected — a `deferred` row
is neither. Two visible consequences: revalidating an older complete bundle
reports its T4 row as `pass → deferred`, a true statement about the current
engine rather than a regression, and an instance whose T4 checker is its own
script keeps today's behaviour until that script declares the marker.
