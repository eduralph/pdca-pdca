Keep ticked plan-advisory findings cleared across iterate-do

After an iterate-do, every plan-advisory finding came back in the next
round's SUMMARY.md §6 as an open, unticked row, even the ones the human had
already ticked. An iterate-do keeps the plan-advisory files, so each new
assembly read them again, and nothing carried the human's tick forward. The
human had to clear the same plan findings again every round, and accept was
blocked until they did.

Now, at an iterate-do, the plan-advisory findings the human ticked in §6
are recorded in plan-advisory-cleared.json in the bundle. Later assemblies
leave those findings out of §6. They are dropped rather than shown as
"- [x]", so assembly never writes a tick that could be read as the human's.
A copy of the finding in the deferred-findings ledger does not bring it
back either.

The record keeps the "plan-advisory-" prefix, so it is removed at the same
time as the plan-advisory evidence: on iterate-plan, and when the plan
advisory runs again. A re-run that raises the same text opens the finding
again. A finding the human never ticked stays open and still blocks accept.
Ticks are read only from the §6 that assembly wrote, so a "- [x]" quoted
elsewhere in the summary clears nothing. If the record cannot be written or
read, the findings show as open, which is the safe side.

Fixes #603
