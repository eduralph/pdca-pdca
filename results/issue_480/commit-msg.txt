fix(leaves): plan review covers the briefs a split writes in session

With a plan reviewer configured, `pdca` reviews each brief a Plan session
writes before anything is built from it. When a single-issue Plan session
split its issue with `pdca split <id> --accept`, that review looked at the
issue the session was started on, not at what the session wrote. The new
child briefs were never reviewed, and they went on to be built as they
were. The parent got one of two wrong outcomes. If it still had its old
brief, the review graded that superseded brief on an issue already closed
as split, and could open a revision session to edit a brief nobody will
build. If the parent had no brief, the review silently did nothing. The
children never got a second chance: they come out of the split already
planned, so the flow skips Plan for them.

This happened twice on a real project, and reviewing the children by hand
afterwards found material errors in their briefs both times.

Both Plan paths now pick what to review the same way. Before the session,
they take a content hash of every brief in the results directory. Afterwards,
they review every brief the session added or changed, wherever it was
written. The multi-issue path already worked like this; the single-issue path
now shares the same two helpers. The review pass itself now also skips any
issue marked closed (`close-disposition`), so a split parent is never
reviewed on either path, even when its brief changed in the same session.
Placeholder briefs are still skipped, and with no plan reviewer configured
Plan behaves as before.

Out of scope: children of a split accepted outside a Plan session, from a
sign-off session or a shell, never enter Plan, so they still get no plan
review.
