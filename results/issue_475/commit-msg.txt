fix(flow): say "no new session" only once the decision is applied

A run picks up a sign-off decision the bundle already carries and applies it
without opening a session (09e4a6677b763380da8f264a2aae947f719c0bcb). It
announced that up front — "applying the '<action>' sign-off decision already
recorded in the bundle; no new session" — before anything had been applied,
so the very next line could contradict it. An accept the C6 guard refuses
(§6 NEEDS-HUMAN still open) does fall through to a fresh sign-off session; a
bundle that lost its SUMMARY.md has its decision dropped; an unsignable
SUMMARY.md is quarantined and the bundle goes back to reassemble. In all
three the operator read a promise and its withdrawal one line apart, on the
path that asks them to come back and judge the bundle again.

The notice is now printed after the apply, and only when the decision really
was recorded. Nothing else moves: the C6 refusal message, the fall-through
to a fresh session, the return values and every state transition are
unchanged, and a decision that genuinely is applied without a session is
still reported in the same terms — on both the single-issue path and the
batch sweep, which share this function.
