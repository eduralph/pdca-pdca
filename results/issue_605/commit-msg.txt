Stop carrying the advisory decorrelation note into later rounds

Under [leaves.advisory_selection] mode = "vendor-complement", a Check that
cannot pick an advisory reviewer from a different vendor than the builder
files a note asking the human to confirm the review's independence by hand.
That note is about that Check's choice of reviewer, not about the patch.

Auto-iterate treated it like any other finding needing human judgment and
held it for handover. So after a rebuild, even when the next Check did find
a different-vendor reviewer and wrote no note, the handover still asked the
human to confirm the independence of a review of a patch that had already
been thrown away.

The note is now handled like a review or gate that gave no verdict: it is
never held for handover. Every Check makes the reviewer choice again, so a
round shows the note exactly when that round wrote it, and it still blocks
accept there until the human ticks it. Other advisory findings are held as
before.

The meaning of NeedsHumanItem.no_verdict is widened to "a row about this
Check's own run", with its comments, the auto-iterate docstrings, the flow's
auto-iterate notice and docs/07-crosscutting.md updated to match.

Fixes #605
