- **Invariant to restore:** In stack mode, a textual conflict between accepted bundles of the same wave costs only the bundles that conflict (and those that depend on them) for this run, never the run, and each is reported with the partner it conflicts with and the file, before the next wave builds. Precisely: taking the wave's bundles in the fold's merge order, a bundle is held exactly when it does not merge cleanly onto the line plus the same-wave bundles already kept, yet does merge cleanly onto the line without them. So a bundle is judged against the bundles actually kept, never against one already held; and a bundle that merges cleanly is never held, whatever files it shares. A bundle that does not merge onto the line even without its same-wave partners conflicts with earlier-wave work, and the fold still stops on it, as today. Source: the harness's own rule for an accepted bundle the fold cannot carry, "Hold it, and via `_runnable` its dependents, rather than stop the run" (#593, `flow.py:2323-2336`, and `_runnable`'s docstring, `flow.py:691-694`); and #674's acceptance criteria. Self-test: catching only the #623/#631 shape (two new class definitions) would not satisfy it; the property is stated over any textual conflict between same-wave branches.
