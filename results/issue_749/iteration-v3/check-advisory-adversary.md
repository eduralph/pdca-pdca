The brief's rule (ii) ("partner's head does not merge onto S alone while the head's own change does") covers both. Only the file count separates them. The one-file case is a safe stop (the pre-#749 behaviour, no wrong advice), and no success-criterion case needs it. The human should decide whether to accept it as a known limit, or ask for a hunk-level comparison (a design change, not a quick fix). It is not worth an auto-iterate round on its own.
